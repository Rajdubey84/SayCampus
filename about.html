About SayCampus

The SayCampus is an innovative digital platform designed to make communication between students and administration transparent, fast, and secure. It ensures every voice is heard and every issue is resolved efficiently.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower students with a simple and fair system to submit complaints, track their progress, and get resolutions without fear or delay.

🚀 Key Features

  • Anonymous and secure complaint submissions.
  • Real-time complaint tracking for users.
  • Admin dashboard for managing complaints efficiently.
  • Category-wise complaints (Hostel, Library, Bus, etc.)
  • Offline support with auto-sync once online.
  • Clean, modern, and responsive user interface.

⚙️ How It Works

Students submit complaints using a web form or QR code. Data syncs automatically with Firebase. Admins review, update, and resolve complaints while users can track status in real-time. This promotes transparency and faster communication between students and management.
